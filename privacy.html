Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026. Applies to the ZEXTA Android app (com.satya.zexta).

Summary

ZEXTA keeps everything on your phone. It has no servers, no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. The developer never receives any of your data.

What ZEXTA stores on your phone

Your Instagram, YouTube, Reels and Shorts time per day; the limits, app rules and weekly commitment you set; your focus sessions; a log of when ZEXTA stepped in (time, app and kind of block, kept 90 days, for "blocked today"); your notification choices and the reminder engine's own counts (which reminders were sent and opened); the name you type (optional); the free-trial start date and whether the lifetime unlock is owned. This stays in ZEXTA's private storage on this phone. It is excluded from cloud backups and is never sent anywhere.

Accessibility service

ZEXTA uses Android's Accessibility service only to block what you chose: it sees which app is open (to count Instagram/YouTube time and apply your app rules, such as a block or daily limit for any app you pick), reads the layout of Instagram and YouTube screens and the web address in supported browsers (to find Reels and Shorts). Only if you switch them on in Settings > Other apps, it also looks at screen structure (view identifiers, not content) in Instagram and YouTube to hide Explore and the Home feed, and in Snapchat and Reddit to close Spotlight and short videos. It can press Back or Home and show a cover over the Reels/Shorts button or over a section you chose to hide. It never stops you turning ZEXTA off, and never saves, records or sends screen content, typed text, passwords or messages.

Usage access

With your permission, ZEXTA reads Android's own screen-time records to show your screen time, unlocks and most-used apps in Insights, keep Instagram and YouTube time accurate, and enforce daily app limits. These numbers stay on your phone.

Notifications

Reminders, summaries and festival greetings are chosen by rules on your phone, from the data above. Nothing is sent to or received from any server for them, and they work without internet. On the lock screen they show no app names or times. Every kind can be switched off in Settings > Notification controls.

Detection report (optional)

If you tap "Copy report" in a protection check, ZEXTA puts a short text on your clipboard (phone model, Android, Instagram and YouTube versions, and which detection checks passed) so you can send it yourself. It contains no usage history.

Installed apps

ZEXTA lists the apps that appear on your home screen so you can choose which ones to block. The list is not stored or shared.

Uninstall protection (optional)

If you turn it on for a commitment, ZEXTA becomes a device administrator through Android's own consent screen. It uses no admin policies: it can't lock the screen, erase data or see passwords. It switches off when your commitment ends, on Sunday with one tap, or automatically if the free trial ends without an unlock.

Friend lock and weekly report (optional)

If you turn on a friend lock, the code itself is never stored: ZEXTA keeps only a salted, one-way hash of it on this phone, plus the number and time of wrong tries, and deletes them when the commitment ends or is changed without the lock. A generated code is passed to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp) only when you tap Send. The weekly report is plain text that ZEXTA hands to the app you choose, only when you tap Share. ZEXTA sends nothing by itself.

Purchases

The lifetime unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's privacy policy; ZEXTA never sees your card, UPI or account details. ZEXTA only asks Google Play whether your account owns the unlock, and checks Google's signature on that answer on the phone. This is the only time ZEXTA uses the internet, and it sends none of your usage data.

Sharing

ZEXTA shares no data with anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software. The only third-party component is Google Play Billing, used solely for the purchase described above.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling ZEXTA, or clearing its storage (when no commitment is locked), permanently deletes everything it stored.

Children

ZEXTA is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact
