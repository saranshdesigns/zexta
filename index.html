Questions

Can ZEXTA read my chats or passwords?

No. It uses Android's Accessibility service only to see which app is open and to find the sections you chose to block, like Reels and Shorts. It never saves, records or sends screen content.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. The only time ZEXTA goes online is when Google Play checks your purchase (Lifetime or a month).

Can I get stuck in a commitment?

No. A commitment always unlocks on Sunday. You can end it early with a 24-hour wait, and with your friend's code if you set a friend lock.

A friend gave me a ZEXTA code. Where do I use it?

Open ZEXTA, go to Settings and tap "Have a code?". You can also redeem it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions, then open ZEXTA.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8.0 and newer, including Android 16.
